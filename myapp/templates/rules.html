ℹ️ About ActiPoints

Apart from technical knowledge and skills, to be successful as professionals, students should have excellent soft skills, leadership qualities, and team spirit. They should also possess entrepreneurial capabilities and societal commitment.

To nurture these qualities, KTU has introduced Activity Points that students must earn during their academic stay at the university. These cover extra-curricular and co-curricular activities.

  • 📌 B.Tech students must earn 100 activity points to qualify for their degree.
  • 📌 Lateral entry students (joining from the 3rd semester) need 75 points.
  • 📌 The points are awarded on a pass/fail basis and carry 2 credits, but do not affect CGPA.
  • 📌 NSS & NCC points can be entered only after completing a 2-year program.

Here are the official guidelines for earning and managing ActiPoints.

✅ Activity Verification

All submitted activities must be verified by the faculty coordinator before points are awarded.

✅ Points Distribution

Each type of activity has a different point value based on achievement, zone, and category.

❌ Plagiarism Policy

If an activity submission is found to be plagiarized, the student will be disqualified from earning ActiPoints.

🔄 Re-evaluation Requests

If you think your points were miscalculated, you can contact admin.
